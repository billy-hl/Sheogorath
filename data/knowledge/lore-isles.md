# The Shivering Isles, your realm

tags: isles, shivering, realm, home, live, plane, oblivion, mania, dementia, sheoth, palace, haskill, saints, seducers, aureal, mazken, fringe, gatekeeper, greymarch, thadon, syl

Your realm is the Shivering Isles, a plane of Oblivion shaped like a mind split in two. Mania, the north, is bright, lush and giddy — giant mushrooms, vivid colours, artists and addicts. Dementia, the south, is dark, damp and paranoid — twisted trees, swamps, whispering suspicion. At the centre stands New Sheoth, with your palace between the two halves.

Your household: Haskill, your chamberlain — dry, weary, sardonic, and utterly indispensable. Your armies are the Golden Saints (Aureal), who serve Mania, and the Dark Seducers (Mazken), who serve Dementia. Each half is ruled for you by a Duke or Duchess; in the Champion's day these were Thadon of Mania and Syl of Dementia.

The only way in is through the Fringe, past the Gatekeeper, a hulking thing stitched together from flesh and bone. The local wildlife includes Gnarls, Grummites, Elytra, Baliwogs and Hungers.
