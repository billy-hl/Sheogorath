/*
 * The Celestial Shovel - a reskin of the vanilla large shovel, with three
 * numbers changed.
 *
 * IT IS THE VANILLA MESH, NOT A NEW ONE.
 * model CelestialShovel points mesh at weapons/2handed/Shovel - the same file
 * the vanilla Shovel uses - and overrides only the texture. That override is
 * how vanilla itself ships variants: HandScytheForged and BallPeenHammerForged
 * both reuse another item's mesh and differ only in this line, and the mesh's
 * own TextureFilename ("Objects_Shovel.png") loses to it. Shipping no mesh
 * means nothing to keep in sync when the base game retopologises a shovel, and
 * the attachment offsets below are copied verbatim from vanilla's model Shovel
 * so it sits in the hand and on the back exactly where a shovel sits.
 *
 * WHAT ACTUALLY DIFFERS FROM Base.Shovel
 *   ConditionMax   10 -> 30   triple durability: three times the hits before
 *                             it breaks.
 *   HeadCondition  10 -> 30   the head wears on its own track in B42, so
 *                             leaving it at 10 would have capped the item's
 *                             real life at vanilla no matter what
 *                             ConditionMax said.
 *   MinDamage     0.8 -> 1.2  1.5x
 *   MaxDamage     1.9 -> 2.85 1.5x
 *   DoorDamage     10 -> 15   1.5x, so "damage" means the same thing against a
 *                             door as against a zombie.
 *
 * Everything else - swing times, ranges, sounds, DigType, tags - is vanilla
 * Shovel to the letter. It digs graves, plows furrows, takes dirt and clears
 * ashes exactly like the shovel it is, and any recipe or action that asks for
 * a shovel by tag accepts it.
 *
 * TreeDamage stays 1. Vanilla's is 1 and the field is an integer, so 1.5x is
 * not expressible; rounding it to 2 would have doubled tree felling off the
 * back of a damage multiplier nobody asked to apply to trees.
 *
 * Researchablerecipes is deliberately dropped. Vanilla's shovel carries
 * MakeSpadeHeadCudgel, Forge_Spade_Head, Forge_Gardening_Trowel and
 * CarveWoodenSpade, and researching a recipe consumes the item. On a common
 * shovel that is a fair trade; on one of these it is a way to destroy the
 * rarest thing on the server by clicking the wrong context entry. The recipes
 * are still learnable from any ordinary shovel, so nothing is lost.
 */
