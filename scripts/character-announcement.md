# 📋 Character Sheets

Want people to know your survivor is a jumpy ex-nurse out of Louisville, and not just a username? Now you can.

## Setting it up — about two minutes, once

**1. Prove the account is yours**
Type `/character link` in Discord. You get a short code only you can see.

**2. Say the code in-game**
Hop on the server and type that code in chat. Any chat works. I'm watching, and I'll spot it within 30 seconds.

**3. Write your character**
Type `/character sheet`. A box pops up with five questions — how old they are, what they did before all this, their story, and how people should approach them out there. Fill it in, hit submit.

Done. Your sheet shows up in <#1536868592342138930>.

## Half of it fills itself in

You don't type your survival time or your skills — I read those straight off the server. So your sheet always says what you've *actually* done, not what you reckon you've done. Type `/character refresh` any time to bring it up to date.

## When you die

Your sheet gets marked ⚰️ **Deceased** and kept, with your eulogy attached. It stays as part of the server's history. Then you write a new one for whoever comes next.

## Looking people up

`/character view <name>` — pull up anyone's sheet
`/character whois @someone` — see which survivor a Discord member is playing

Worth a look before you walk up on a stranger holding a shotgun.

*Didn't get a DM after saying your code? It probably still worked — some people have DMs from server members switched off. Just run `/character sheet` and see.*
