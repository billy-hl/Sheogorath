# 🔧 Mod update

The server restarted this morning with some mod changes. There was a stretch afterwards where nobody could get in — **that is fixed now**, and you should be able to join normally.

**Let Steam finish updating your mods before you launch the game.** If the loading screen sits on "Downloading" for a long time, quit, let Steam finish in the background, and come back.

## Added

**Propane Exchange Cabinets** — swap empty propane tanks for full ones at the cabinets outside stores.
**LG Extended Plumbing** — plumb sinks, toilets and other fixtures properly.
**LG Connect To Water Supply** — hook a building up to the mains while the water is still running.
**Named skill VHS tapes** — tapes now tell you which skill they teach, instead of you finding out after watching.

## Removed

**Wandering Zombies** — it was not doing anything. Gone.

## Corrections to the earlier version of this post

**Smarter Storage is NOT installed.** It was listed here by mistake and has been taken back out. If your game auto-subscribed to it while you were trying to join, you can unsubscribe.

**Waterpipes is staying after all.** It was going to be replaced, but removing it now would break the save, so it stays. Carry on using it.

## Worth knowing

Both LG plumbing mods are new. **Connect To Water Supply** has a known bug on dedicated servers where the connect option does not always appear — the author is aware and working on it.

If any of the new mods misbehave, post in the mod requests forum rather than assuming it is on your end.
